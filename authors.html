Review & publication

A transparent single-blind review process, equal standing for oral and poster contributions, and open-access proceedings.

At least two reviewersSingle-blindOpen access
Peer review

How papers are assessed

Reviewers know the authors’ identities; reviewer identities are not disclosed to authors.

Every full paper will be evaluated by at least two reviewers. Authors should include their names, affiliations, and contact information in the paper.

Evaluation criteria

  • Relevance to the workshop
  • Clarity and quality of presentation
  • Soundness of the methodology or experience analysis
  • Originality or practical significance
  • Quality of the evidence and discussion
  • Potential usefulness for the university community

Conflicts of interest

Conflicts will be handled by the Workshop Chairs. Committee members who are authors of a submission will not participate in its review, discussion, or acceptance decision.

At the workshop

Oral and poster presentations

The organizers will select the presentation format based on the contribution and the overall program.

Oral

Present to the room

Accepted contributions may be scheduled for an oral presentation and discussion during the workshop.

Poster

Discuss in depth

Accepted contributions may be assigned a poster presentation for focused exchange with workshop participants.

Oral and poster papers are equally accepted contributions and are included in the proceedings under the same publication conditions. At least one author of every accepted paper must attend and present.

Proceedings

Open and independently published

Accepted papers will be published in an open-access proceedings volume with an ISBN assigned in collaboration with the Politecnico di Torino Library System.

Publication is conditional on submitting the final camera-ready version and the required publication agreement. The proceedings license and detailed camera-ready instructions will be communicated before final submission.

The PoliTO Community Workshop is intended as a recurring initiative, with a separate proceedings volume for each annual edition.

Important clarification

The workshop uses the Springer LNCS single-column format for layout only. The proceedings are published independently by Politecnico di Torino and are not affiliated with Springer Nature.
