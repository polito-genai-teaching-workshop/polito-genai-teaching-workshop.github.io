Submission route

Two stages,
one paper

Only contributions registered by the abstract deadline may proceed to full-paper submission.

Stage 1 · Due 1 December 2026

Abstract registration

Register a 300–500 word abstract in English directly through Microsoft Forms. No abstract file or PDF upload is required at this stage.

Abstracts are used for planning and reviewer assignment, not for a separate acceptance decision. Authors will receive initial feedback on their contribution by 15 December 2026.

Stage 2 · Due 15 January 2027

Full-paper submission

Upload an original full paper in English as a PDF. It must be 8–12 pages long, including references and appendices, and follow the official workshop template once released.

The full-paper platform and link will be announced on this website and emailed to registered authors no later than the initial-feedback date, 15 December 2026.

Stage 1 checklist

What the abstract must include

Give the organizers enough information to understand the proposed contribution and identify suitable reviewers.

  • Title, author names, affiliations, and contact details
  • One to five keywords
  • The educational context or problem
  • The objectives or questions addressed
  • The proposed or implemented approach
  • Expected or available findings, evidence, or lessons learned
  • The contribution’s relevance to the workshop
Paper format

Prepare the full paper

Length and language

Full papers must be written in English and contain 8–12 pages, including references and appendices.

Template

The official paper template is currently being revised and is not yet available. It will be published on this page before full-paper submissions open.

Paper template not yet available

Originality

Submissions must present original work that has not already been formally published and is not simultaneously under review elsewhere.

A previous presentation in a non-archival seminar or workshop does not preclude submission, but it must be disclosed to the organizers.

Author information

The review is single-blind. Include author names, affiliations, and contact information in the submitted paper.

Before you submit

Final check

The Microsoft Forms link will be published here as soon as abstract registration opens.

Registered on time

Your abstract is registered through Microsoft Forms by 1 December 2026.

Within format

Your PDF is in English, 8–12 pages inclusive, and uses the official template once it is released.

Original and attributed

The work is original, author details are present, and prior non-archival presentations are disclosed.
