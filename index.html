Why this workshop

A forum for thoughtful, evidence-led exchange

Generative AI is changing how courses are designed, taught, assessed, and experienced. This workshop creates space to compare what works, what fails, and what we still need to understand.

01

Share practice

Bring a research study, teaching experience, case study, method, tool, educational resource, position paper, or institutional initiative.

02

Examine critically

Discuss reliability, integrity, privacy, inclusion, sustainability, and the effects of AI on autonomy, creativity, and learning.

03

Connect across PoliTO

Meet colleagues and students across engineering, architecture, design, and the university’s professional communities.

At a glance

One contribution,
two stages

By 1 December 2026

Register a 300–500 word abstract

Enter the abstract directly in Microsoft Forms. It is used to understand the contribution and identify reviewers; no file upload is required. Initial feedback will be provided by 15 December.

By 15 January 2027

Upload an 8–12 page full paper

Submit an original paper in English as a PDF, using the official workshop template once released. The platform will be announced by the initial-feedback date.

Single-blind review

Present and publish

Accepted papers will be presented as an oral or poster contribution and published in open-access PoliTO proceedings, subject to final requirements.

Bring your experience into the conversation.

Successful experiments, critical evaluations, and lessons from failure are all welcome.
